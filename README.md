📊 Sales Performance & Customer Insights Analysis
📌 Overview

This project analyzes a retail sales dataset to uncover key revenue drivers, customer trends, and business insights using Python.

🧰 Tools Used
Python
Pandas
Matplotlib
📂 Dataset
Retail sales dataset containing order, customer, and product information
🔍 Key Analysis Performed
Sales trend analysis over time
Top-performing products identification
Regional sales comparison
Customer segment analysis
📈 Key Insights
A small number of products contribute significantly to total revenue
Sales are concentrated in specific regions and cities
Certain customer segments drive majority of sales
Monthly trends indicate seasonal variations in sales
💡 Business Recommendations
Focus marketing efforts on top-performing products
Improve strategies in low-performing regions
Target high-value customer segments for growth
